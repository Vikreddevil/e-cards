## 1. Name, tagline and domains

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| **Name** | **CardReel**, written as one word with a capital R. Never "Card Reel", "Cardreel" or "CardReels" |
| **Meaning** | **Card** (image greetings and invitations) + **Reel** (short videos). One name for both formats, every occasion |
| **Local spellings** | Hindi/Marathi **कार्डरील** · Tamil **கார்டுரீல்** (used in regional marketing; the logo stays Latin) |
| **Tagline (EN)** | "Wishes, Cards & Video Invitations for every occasion" |
| **Short line** | "Make it personal. Share it in a minute." |
| **Primary domain** | **`cardreel.in`** (founder confirmed it's available). Register it in the founder's name **exactly as on the PAN** (`12` §1.2). Turn on auto-renew and two-step login |
| **Optional redirects** | `cardreel.co.in`, `cardreal.in` (people who hear "card real"), only if cheap |
| **`cardreel.com`** | Parked and listed for sale. Revisit after revenue; not needed for an India-first launch |

**Watch-outs:**
- **"Card reel" is also a physical product:** the retractable clip for ID badges. Early searches for "cardreel" will show some badge-holder shops. That fades as branded searches ("CardReel Diwali wishes") grow.
- **"Reels" is Instagram's feature name.** Use **"Reel"** only, and never copy Instagram's colours or icon style.

**Tagline translations** (adapt rather than translate word for word; native-speaker review required before use):
- हिन्दी: "हर मौक़े के लिए शुभकामनाएँ, कार्ड और वीडियो निमंत्रण"
- मराठी: "प्रत्येक प्रसंगासाठी शुभेच्छा, कार्ड आणि व्हिडिओ निमंत्रण"
- தமிழ்: "ஒவ்வொரு நிகழ்வுக்கும் வாழ்த்துகள், கார்டுகள், வீடியோ அழைப்பிதழ்கள்"

## 3. Trademark (deferred by the founder)

| Step | When | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Free search** | Before launch | IP India public trademark search for **CARDREEL**, **CARD REEL** and **CARDREAL**. Look for similar marks in classes 9, 38 and 42 |
| **File** | Later, once there's traction | **Class 42** first (online design tool for cards and videos), then **9** (app/software) and **38** (sending greeting cards and messages online). About **₹4,500 per class** online for an individual or small business **[confirm on IP India]** |
| **Symbols** | — | **™** once filed; **®** only after registration (often months to 1–2 years). Valid 10 years, renewable |

Without registration you still have limited rights from using the name first ("passing off"), but enforcing them is slow and costly. Filing early makes takedowns of fake "CardReel" pages much easier.

## 4. Logo

**Concept:** a festive card whose top and bottom edges carry **film sprocket holes**, so the card is also a frame of film. Inside it, a **marigold diya flame** points right and **doubles as a play button**. One mark says *card*, *video* and *celebration*. It carries **no religious symbol**, so it works equally for Diwali, Eid, Christmas, birthdays and weddings.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `brand/logo-icon.svg` | App icon, social avatars, watermark on free cards (light backgrounds) |
| `brand/logo-icon-dark.svg` | The same on dark backgrounds (card colour `#8B5CF6`) |
| `brand/logo-wordmark.svg` | Website header, emails, end card (light backgrounds) |
| `brand/logo-wordmark-dark.svg` | The same on dark backgrounds |
| `brand/favicon.svg` | Browser tab (simplified: 3 holes per edge) |

**Rules:**
- **Clear space** around the mark equals the height of one sprocket hole × 2.
- **Minimum size:** 20px for the icon on screen, 96px wide for the wordmark.
- **Wordmark colours:** "Card" in Plum and "Reel" in Violet (light text and light violet in dark mode).
- **Don't** recolour the flame, add outlines or shadows, place the icon on busy photos without its card shape, or stretch it.
- The SVG wordmark uses live text in **Baloo 2 Bold**. **Convert it to outlines** before printing or uploading to app stores.

**Free-output branding (Phase 0):**
- a small icon in the bottom corner of free cards;
- a **2-second end card** on free videos: the icon's flame flickers once, then "Made with CardReel — make yours free · cardreel.in".

## 5. Colour and type

### 5.1 Brand palette: "Purple & Marigold" (chosen)
Modern and fresh, with a festive Indian feel: a royal violet with marigold (the flame) and mint (highlights). The founder chose it on the brand board over Teal & Coral, Pink Pop and Cobalt & Lime.

| Token | Role | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--violet` | Primary: buttons, links, logo card, "Reel" in the wordmark | `#6D28D9` | `#B69CFF` (text/buttons) · `#8B5CF6` (logo card) |
| `--marigold` | Accent: the flame, badges. **Fill only, always with dark text** | `#FFB627` | `#FFB627` |
| `--mint` | Highlight: offers, "New" tags. **Fill only, always with dark text** | `#5EEAD4` | `#5EEAD4` |
| `--lavender` / `--night` | Page background | `#FBF9FF` | `#151022` |
| `--plum` | Body text, "Card" in the wordmark | `#1C1530` | `#F3EFFB` |
| `--muted` | Secondary text | `#5A5270` | `#B7AECB` |

**Contrast (WCAG 2.1; AA needs ≥ 4.5:1 for text, ≥ 3:1 for graphics):**

| Pair | Ratio | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Plum on Lavender | **16.8:1** | Body text |
| Violet on Lavender | **6.8:1** | Links, headings |
| White on Violet | **7.1:1** | Button labels |
| Muted on Lavender | **7.0:1** | Secondary text |
| Plum on Marigold | **10.0:1** | Badges |
| Plum on Mint | **11.8:1** | "New" tags |
| Marigold or Mint as text on light backgrounds | — | ❌ never; fills only |
| Light text `#F3EFFB` on Night (dark mode) | **16.4:1** | Body text |
| Light violet `#B69CFF` on Night | **8.1:1** | Links, buttons |
| Night on light violet | **8.1:1** | Button labels (dark mode) |
| Logo card `#8B5CF6` vs `#111418` | **4.4:1** | Graphic |

**Keep it distinct from other purple brands.** PhonePe uses a deep indigo with white, and Meesho a pink-purple. CardReel uses a brighter violet, **always paired with marigold and mint**, in solid colours. **No purple-to-blue gradients.**

**Festival themes** sit on top of the brand palette, one per collection (see `03` §7): Diwali maroon + gold, Holi multi-colour on white, Pongal green + turmeric, Gudi Padwa saffron + green, Eid emerald + ivory, Christmas red + pine, Weddings rani pink + gold. The brand colours stay fixed in the header, buttons and logo.

## 6. SEO principle (why the name isn't the SEO lever)

**The name is chosen for brand value:** memorable, trustworthy in a WhatsApp preview, easy across languages, and ownable. Rankings come from the site:
1. **A landing-page grid by festival × language × need**, each page with real text wishes, templates and FAQs:
   - "Diwali wishes in Marathi"
   - "birthday wishes with name and photo"
   - "wedding video invitation in Tamil"
   - "griha pravesh invitation in Hindi"
2. **Publish 4–6 weeks before each festival.** Search demand builds weeks ahead.
3. **Technical basics:**
   - `.in` domain (the clearest India signal since Search Console's country targeting was removed in 2022);
   - hreflang across `hi/en/mr/ta`;
   - clean Latin URLs (`/mr/gudi-padwa-wishes`);
   - fast mobile pages (Core Web Vitals);
   - `VideoObject` / `ImageObject` structured data.
4. **Titles carry the keywords**, e.g. *"Diwali Wishes with Name & Photo | CardReel"*, *"Wedding Video Invitation in Tamil | CardReel"*.
5. **Privacy:** personal card and event pages are **noindex**. They still bring brand searches and links.
6. **YouTube Shorts and Pinterest** of templates bring traffic from video and image search.
7. **Measure** with Search Console and Google Trends India, and write the next festival pages from that data.

## 7. Voice

- **Warm, short, respectful.** Hindi/Marathi use *aap / tumhi*; Tamil uses *neenga*.
- **Say what happens:** "Make your card", "Send on WhatsApp", "Notify me when Premium launches".
- **Trust lines** appear where money or links are involved: "No app needed", "Safe link: no app, no login", "Auto-refund if anything fails" (Phase 1).
- **No guilt or fear** in copy, and no fake urgency (see the dark-pattern rules in `03` §4).
